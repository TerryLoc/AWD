With over 15 years in video production — directing, shooting, and editing across documentary, commercial, and branded content — I know how to tell a story, frame a shot, and execute with precision. Those same instincts now drive how I build for the web.

Based near Ashford Studios in Wicklow, Ireland, I completed my Full Stack Development Diploma at Code Institute and I'm now working as a freelance Digital Architect. Additionally, as the in-house Technical Lead at High Tide Studios, I offer full access to a premium podcast and vodcast studio, equipped with multi-camera and greenscreen setups.

I specialise in the frontend — clean interfaces, sharp detail, intuitive flow — but I'm equally comfortable in the backend, building with Python, Django, and SQL. When video and web intersect, that's where I'm most at home.

How I Work

The
Process.

Same discipline whether we're building a site or cutting a film. Every project follows the same four-stage blueprint.

01 — Discovery

Brief & Strategy

We define goals, audience, and deliverables. Whether it's a web brief or a shoot plan — clarity up front saves time everywhere else.

02 — Architecture

Design & Pre-Production

Wireframes, storyboards, or both. The structure is designed before a single frame is shot or a line of code is written.

03 — Build

Create & Produce

Execution in full — development sprints, on-location production, editing passes. You're kept in the loop throughout.

04 — Launch

Deliver & Iterate

Final delivery with handover documentation. For ongoing clients: retainer support, updates, and continued creative partnership.
